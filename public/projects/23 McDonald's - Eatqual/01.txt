# Headline: McDonald's Eatqual
Mcdonald’s believes that everyone deserves to enjoy the simple pleasures of life, like biting into a delicious burger. That’s why we created Eatqual, a special packaging that makes it easier for people with limited hand mobility to hold and eat our burgers. Eatqual is not just a product, it’s a movement. A movement that aims to raise awareness and empathy for the differently-abled community in India. A movement that invites you to join us in making delicious feel-good moments easy for everyone. In 2022, we launched a series of campaigns to promote Eatqual and celebrate the stories of our customers who overcame their challenges with Eatqual.Mcdonald’s Eatqual - 2022 is more than just a campaign. It’s a commitment. A commitment to make our burgers accessible for all.
